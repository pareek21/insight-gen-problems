1. WoW 698

Fajtlowicz, Written on the Wall #698 (2004). Proved July 2026 by combining two 1993 lemmas. True.
Reference proof: 656 tokens. Statement written for this study from the Written on the Wall conjecture.

Problem

Let $G$ be a finite simple graph with $m$ edges, and let $\lambda_1 \ge \lambda_2 \ge \dots \ge \lambda_n$ be the eigenvalues of its adjacency matrix, listed with multiplicity. Define

$$s^-(G) = \Big(\sum_{i:\ \lambda_i < 0} \lambda_i^2\Big)^{1/2},$$

the Euclidean norm of the negative adjacency eigenvalues ($s^-(G) = 0$ if there are none), and the Randić index

$$R(G) = \sum_{uv \in E(G)} \frac{1}{\sqrt{d(u)\,d(v)}},$$

where $d(u)$ is the degree of $u$ and the sum runs over the edges of $G$ ($R(G) = 0$ if $G$ has no edges).

Prove that $s^-(G) \le R(G)$ for every finite simple graph $G$.

Hints

Hand-written · shortest that worked

14 tokens (2.1% of the proof) · N 2/2

First prove \(\lambda_1R(G)\ge m\).

Standard, 50-token budget

45 tokens (6.9% of the proof) · N 4/4

First prove \(\lambda_1R(G)\ge m\), using the Rayleigh quotient at the vector of square-root degrees and Cauchy–Schwarz over edges. Combine this product bound with the sum of squared eigenvalues.

Standard, 100-token budget

90 tokens (14% of the proof) · N 4/4

For a graph with edges, obtain \(\lambda_1R(G)\ge m\) by testing the Rayleigh quotient on square-root degrees and applying Cauchy–Schwarz over edges. Then \(\lambda_1^2+R(G)^2\ge2m\). The trace identity says the squared eigenvalues sum to \(2m\); subtracting just the positive contribution \(\lambda_1^2\) already bounds the sum of negative squares from above.
