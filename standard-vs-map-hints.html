Average sensitivity of PTFs

Reference proof 5,509 tokens · no hint: 0/4 correct †

Problem statement

A polynomial threshold function assigns a sign to each vertex of the Boolean cube by evaluating a real polynomial. On the cube, the identities $x_i^2=1$ allow every polynomial to be replaced by a multilinear polynomial of no larger degree without changing its values. Average sensitivity measures the expected number of coordinate changes that reverse that sign. For $x\in\{-1,1\}^n$, let $x^{\oplus i}$ be obtained by reversing coordinate $i$. For $f:\{-1,1\}^n\to\{-1,1\}$, define $$I(f)=\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbb P\{f(X)\ne f(X^{\oplus i})\},\qquad X\text{ uniform on }\{-1,1\}^n.$$ This quantity is also called the total influence of $f$. Throughout, $$\operatorname{sgn}(t)=\begin{cases}1,&t\ge0,\\-1,&t<0.\end{cases}$$ Determine whether there is an absolute constant $C>0$ with the following property: for all integers $n\ge1$ and $1\le d\le n$, and every real multilinear polynomial $p$ in $n$ variables of degree at most $d$, the function $f:\{-1,1\}^n\to\{-1,1\}$ defined by $f(x)=\operatorname{sgn}(p(x))$, with $\operatorname{sgn}(0)=1$, satisfies $$I(f)\le C\,d\sqrt n.$$ The polynomial may vanish on the cube, and no regularity condition is imposed on $p$. Prove your answer.

Problem statement

For a positive integer $n$, write $[n]=\{1,\ldots,n\}$. If $x\in\{0,1\}^n$ and $B\subseteq[n]$, let $x^B$ be obtained by flipping precisely the coordinates in $B$; abbreviate $x^{\{a\}}$ to $x^a$.
