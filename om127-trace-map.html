Where the thinking went

Each bar is one attempt's reasoning, split by what Sol says it was working on.

How this was made. Each attempt's reasoning, and its final answer where it gave one, was cut into windows of about 24,000 characters (186 in all). GPT-6.1 Sol (pro) read each window with the problem, the hint that attempt saw, the 16 claims of Astra's map of the reference proof and a catalogue of other approaches, and listed in order what the window works on, what it does with it (explores, establishes, uses the hint, doubts, abandons, writes up), its rough share of the window and a verbatim quote. These are one model's readings of very long traces, not ground truth.

A node counts as reached by an attempt when it gets at least 0.4% of the attempt's reasoning (at least 2,000 characters), or when the attempt establishes it, takes it from the hint or writes it up. The drawn walks skip passing mentions under 4,000 characters. The r1 and r2 attempts were all stopped at Moonshot's one-hour limit, so their walks end mid-thought; each r1 attempt shown is the first of its three tries. Hint texts are exactly as Kimi saw them.
