5. #127 PTF sensitivity

Gotsman–Linial conjecture (1994), up to a constant. OpenAI release, Sept 2026. True.
Reference proof: 5,509 tokens. Statement: the definitions and theorem from OpenAI's math release (commit 1581d4c, Apache-2.0), posed as “Determine whether …” so that the answer isn't given away.

Problem

A polynomial threshold function assigns a sign to each vertex of the Boolean cube by evaluating a real polynomial. On the cube, the identities $x_i^2=1$ allow every polynomial to be replaced by a multilinear polynomial of no larger degree without changing its values. Average sensitivity measures the expected number of coordinate changes that reverse that sign. For $x\in\{-1,1\}^n$, let $x^{\oplus i}$ be obtained by reversing coordinate $i$. For $f:\{-1,1\}^n\to\{-1,1\}$, define

$$I(f)=\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbb P\{f(X)\ne f(X^{\oplus i})\},\qquad X\text{ uniform on }\{-1,1\}^n.$$

This quantity is also called the total influence of $f$. Throughout,

$$\operatorname{sgn}(t)=\begin{cases}1,&t\ge0,\\-1,&t<0.\end{cases}$$

Determine whether there is an absolute constant $C>0$ with the following property: for all integers $n\ge1$ and $1\le d\le n$, and every real multilinear polynomial $p$ in $n$ variables of degree at most $d$, the function $f:\{-1,1\}^n\to\{-1,1\}$ defined by $f(x)=\operatorname{sgn}(p(x))$, with $\operatorname{sgn}(0)=1$, satisfies

$$I(f)\le C\,d\sqrt n.$$

The polynomial may vanish on the cube, and no regularity condition is imposed on $p$. Prove your answer.

Weight by \(|p|\) and twist by parity to turn degree into a one-sided constraint on a binomial coupling. Remove zeros by a sign-preserving positive constant shift. In counting \(\ell^2\), orthogonalize \(\sqrt{|p|}V_k\), where \(V_k\) is the Fourier degree-\(\le k\) space. Let \(\Pi_k\) be the successive layer projections, set \(N=2^n\), \(M=\sum_k(k-n/2)\Pi_k\), and let \(H\) multiply by \(f(x)\prod_i x_i\). The layer dimensions are \(\binom nk\), giving \(\|M\|_{\rm HS}^2=nN/4\).

For coordinate multiplication \(Z_i\), prove tridiagonality in the grades and \[ \|[M,Z_i]\|_{\rm op}\le1,\qquad \|[M,Z_i]\|_{\rm HS}^2\le N. \] For the operator bound, use grade parity and phases \(\mathrm i^k\). The vertex entries then give \(|M_{xy}|\le1/2\) off the diagonal and show that energy at distance at least two is at most diagonal energy \(A_0\). Hence the total edge deficit from \(1/4\) is at most \(2A_0\). The anticommutator detects diagonal energy and equal-sign edges of parity times \(f\), yielding \(NI(f)\le2\|MH+HM\|_{\rm HS}^2\).

Define \(\mathbb P(R=r,S=s)=N^{-1}\|\Pi_sH\Pi_r\|_{\rm HS}^2\). Unitarity gives binomial marginals. The blocks vanish for \(r+s<n-d\), since weighted pairings involve parity times \(p\), whose Fourier degrees are at least \(n-d\). Moreover, \[ N^{-1}\|MH+HM\|_{\rm HS}^2=\mathbb E(R+S-n)^2. \] Finally, equal-law variables with mean \(m\), standard deviation \(\sigma\), and \(V-U\le a\) satisfy \(\mathbb E(U-V)^2\le8a\sigma\). Prove this using \(g(t)=(t-m)|t-m|/2\): its absolute increments dominate squared displacement divided by four, while equal marginals balance positive and negative increments. Bound the positive increments using \(V-U\le a\). Apply this with \(U=S,V=n-R,a=d,\sigma=\sqrt n/2\), obtaining \(C=8\).
