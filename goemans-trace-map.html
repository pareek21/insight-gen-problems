Where the thinking went

Each bar is one attempt's reasoning, split by what Sol says it was working on.

How this was made. Each attempt's reasoning, and its final answer where it gave one, was cut into windows of about 24,000 characters (98 in all). GPT-6.1 Sol (pro) read each window with the problem, the hint that attempt saw, the 7 claims of Astra's map of the reference proof and a catalogue of other approaches, and listed in order what the window works on, what it does with it (explores, establishes, uses the hint, doubts, abandons, writes up), its rough share of the window and a verbatim quote. These are one model's readings of very long traces, not ground truth.

A node counts as reached by an attempt when it gets at least 0.4% of the attempt's reasoning (at least 2,000 characters), or when the attempt establishes it, takes it from the hint or writes it up. The drawn walks skip passing mentions under 4,000 characters. Round 0 shows three attempts: the fourth (s1) never finished in eight tries, each cut at the one-hour limit or dropped by the provider, and counts as a failure. r1's s0 was stopped at the one-hour limit. Hint texts are exactly as Kimi saw them; a hint's token count is how many tokens it adds to Kimi's prompt.
