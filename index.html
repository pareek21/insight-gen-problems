Problems and hints

The eight research problems in our experiments on how much of a proof a language model needs as a hint before it can prove the result, with the hints from the summary tables. Problems 5–8 come from OpenAI's math release. Hints were written by GPT-6 Astra (pro) from the reference proof.

N = Nemotron 3 Ultra (graded by 3 judges, majority vote); K = Kimi K3 (1 judge); A = GPT-6 Astra pro (1 judge, which also grades its own answers). Scores are correct answers out of attempts. Hint sizes are exact token counts with Nemotron's tokenizer, and shares are of the reference proof's length.
